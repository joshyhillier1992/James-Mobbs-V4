JM
James Mobbs Creative Director · Motion Designer

James started his career in broadcast television, cutting his teeth on identity packages for Channel 4 and ITV before moving into the live music world. That cross-disciplinary background — one foot in the frame-perfect world of broadcast, the other in the high-stakes spontaneity of live events — is what makes his work stand apart.

Over the past decade he has become one of the UK's most trusted motion designers for the music and entertainment industry, delivering LED wall content, show packages and real-time generative visuals for artists, labels and broadcasters alike. His work has appeared on stages at Glastonbury, the BRIT Awards and arenas across Europe.

When he's not on-site with a touring production, James works from his studio in Manchester, collaborating remotely with production companies, creative agencies and brands who need cinematic motion work delivered to broadcast standards and tight deadlines.

The work is always personal. James doesn't outsource, doesn't have a team of juniors doing the heavy lifting — every project is crafted by him, start to finish. That means clients get direct access to the person with the ideas, not an account manager who passes notes down a chain.

What I Do

Broadcast Motion

Channel idents, title sequences, lower thirds and full broadcast design packages — built to spec and delivered broadcast-ready.

Live Show Packages

End-to-end content production for live music events — LED wall content, stage idents, countdowns, intros and real-time playback systems.

Real-Time Visuals

Generative and reactive content using TouchDesigner and Notch — visuals that respond to audio, MIDI or live inputs in real time.

Brand Launch Films

Cinematic reveal films and launch content that make the first impression count — from concept through to final delivery.

Event Screen Content

On-site screen design and content production for conferences, activations and award ceremonies — including same-day delivery.

Creative Direction

Big-picture thinking alongside the craft — helping productions define their visual language before a single frame is made.

How I Work

01

Discover

A deep-dive into your brief, brand and audience. No templates — every project starts with questions.

02

Concept

Visual concepts presented as storyboards, style frames or lo-fi tests — so you see the idea before committing to production.

03

Build

Production in After Effects, Cinema 4D or TouchDesigner, with regular check-ins and draft previews throughout.

04

Deliver

Files exported to your exact spec — broadcast-safe, properly labelled and on time. On-site support available for live events.
