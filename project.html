Live Visuals Show Package Broadcast

Hits Radio
Immersive Experience

Client Hits Radio / Bauer Media Year 2024 Role Creative Director & Motion Designer Duration 6 weeks

The Brief

Hits Radio had just completed a bold visual rebrand and needed a full live show package to match. The brief was simple: make the audience feel the music before a note is played. Every screen, every transition, every ident had to pulse with the station's new energy.

Working directly with the Bauer Media creative team, James developed a system of generative visuals that could react to the live music in real time — ensuring no two performances ever looked identical, while maintaining a coherent visual language across all screens.

The package shipped across seven UK arena dates, with content delivered for LED walls spanning over 400 square metres per venue.

Motion at Scale

The visual system was built in After Effects and TouchDesigner, allowing real-time parameter control from the FOH position. Each scene could shift hue, tempo and density in sync with the DJ set — making the LED walls a live instrument in their own right.

Outputs included 4K loops, real-time MIDI-triggered content and a complete broadcast-safe version for television.

On the Night

From load-in to doors open, James was on-site at every show, running content playback and handling last-minute creative changes in real time. The system was designed to fail gracefully — with hot-swappable backup clips for every scene.
